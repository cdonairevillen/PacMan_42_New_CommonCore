### Collaboration


Although responsibilities were divided, most important decisions were discussed and validated together.

Several systems, especially the GameManager integration, gameplay fixes, and final bug resolution, required collaboration between both team members.

The last week was particularly collaborative; we both worked together to identify, reproduce, and fix gameplay bugs, ensuring a stable and polished final product.
